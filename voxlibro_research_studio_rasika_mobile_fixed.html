Research foundation

Why this study matters

The activities are built around the app's user need, likely usability problems and the research questions in your project brief.

💡

Research need

  • Understand how easily readers find document import and audio tools.
  • Explore how playback speed, voice and navigation affect reading flow.
  • Check reading comfort across text sizes and contrast choices.
  • Identify accessibility expectations for keyboard, screen reader and low-vision use.
  • Collect user priorities to guide a clear, evidence-based redesign.
🧩

Problem areas to investigate

  • Important actions may be difficult to discover or labelled ambiguously.
  • Playback state or speed changes may not be communicated clearly.
  • Text may feel too small, crowded or low-contrast for some readers.
  • Accessibility settings may be hard to find or incompatible with assistive technology.
  • Users may face friction when importing a file, recovering from an error or resuming a session.

These are research hypotheses to test—not confirmed defects in VoxLibro.
